Welcome! I am a PhD candidate at the Carl Albert Congressional Research and Studies
Center at the University of Oklahoma. I study legislative politics, the ideologies and
influence of partisan campaign activists, and public opinion particularly as it relates to
legislative institutions and science and technology policy.

My research has been published in the Election Law Journal.

I have earned a B.A. with honors, Magna Cum Laude in Political Science at Calvin University.
